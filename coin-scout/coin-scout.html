How Coin Scout works
  1. Every 30 seconds it pulls the newest Solana trading pools from GeckoTerminal. Anything older than your age limit is skipped instantly.
  2. Quick checks (free): age, liquidity, market cap, trading volume, whether buyers outnumber sellers, and whether it already pumped.
  3. Safety checks (RugCheck): can the creator still print coins or freeze wallets, is the liquidity locked, do a few wallets own too much.
  4. Deep check (your Token Detective, needs a Helius key): finds wallets secretly linked to each other or to the creator, and launch-block bundles.
  5. If a coin passes everything, it makes a fake buy priced with a real Jupiter quote (what you'd actually get, and which exchange it goes through), then watches it every few seconds and sells by your rules: either a quick flip at a set dollar profit, or letting winners run.
  6. Deployer blacklist: when a coin rugs, the wallet that made it is blocked (and whoever funded that wallet). Deployers whose coins keep crashing get blocked too.
  7. Coins that fail only because they're too new or too quiet get rechecked every few minutes for about 90 minutes. The deep check runs in the background (one coin at a time, 2.5-minute limit) so scouting never pauses.
  8. Leave this tab open in its own window (not minimized). Browsers slow down hidden tabs, and nothing runs while the page is closed.
